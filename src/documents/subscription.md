# Subscription Policy

_Last updated: May 2026_

## 1. Overview

Legendary Profiles offers optional subscription plans that provide enhanced features beyond the free tier. This Subscription Policy explains how subscriptions work, what benefits they include, how billing is handled, and how cancellations take effect.

By subscribing, you agree to this policy as part of our Terms of Service.

---

## 2. Subscription Benefits

Subscription features may include:

- Editable link hubs for your QR code
- Access to analytics dashboards
- Additional customization options
- Priority feature access
- Optional free monthly mints (if offered)

Features may vary by plan and may change over time as the platform evolves.

---

## 3. Billing & Renewal

Subscriptions are billed through third‑party payment processors.

### 3.1 Billing Cycles

You may choose:

- **Monthly billing**
- **Annual billing** (discounted rate)

Your subscription will renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle unless canceled.

### 3.2 Payment Information

You must provide accurate and up‑to‑date payment information.  
If a payment fails, we may:

- Retry the charge
- Suspend subscription features
- Cancel the subscription

---

## 4. Free Monthly Mints (If Applicable)

Some subscription plans may include free monthly mints.

### 4.1 Usage Rules

- Free mints do **not** roll over to future months
- Free mints apply only to the current billing cycle
- Unused free mints are forfeited at the end of the cycle

### 4.2 Additional Mints

Additional mints beyond the free allotment are billed at the standard minting rate.

---

## 5. Changes to Subscription Plans

We may modify subscription features, pricing, or plan structure.  
If changes affect your current plan:

- We will notify you in advance
- Continued use of the subscription constitutes acceptance of the updated terms

---

## 6. Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time.

### 6.1 After Cancellation

When you cancel:

- Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period
- You will not be charged again
- You retain access to subscription features until the period ends

### 6.2 After Expiration

Once the subscription period ends:

- Analytics access is removed
- Link‑hub editing may be restricted
- Free monthly mints stop
- Previously minted cards remain accessible

Your account is not deleted when you cancel.

---

## 7. Refunds

Subscription fees are **non‑refundable**, including:

- Partial billing periods
- Unused subscription time
- Accidental renewals

Refunds may be issued only where required by law.

---

## 8. Account Suspension

We may suspend or terminate subscription access if:

- Payments fail repeatedly
- You violate our Terms of Service
- Fraud or abuse is detected

Suspension does not automatically cancel your subscription; you remain responsible for outstanding charges.

---

## 9. Data Retention

After a subscription ends:

- Minted cards remain accessible
- Analytics data may be retained in anonymized form
- Personal information is retained according to our Privacy Policy
