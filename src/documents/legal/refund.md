# Refund Policy
Last updated: May 2026

## 1. Overview

Legendary Profiles offers both digital goods (minted cards) and physical goods (printed card packs).  
Because these product types function differently, they follow different refund rules.

This Refund Policy explains when refunds, reprints, or credits may be issued.

---

## 2. Digital Goods (Minted Cards)

Minting a card is a **non‑refundable** digital purchase.

When you mint a card:

- The visual design becomes permanently locked
- A unique QR code is generated
- A digital proof is delivered instantly
- The card becomes part of your account

Because digital goods cannot be “returned,” all minting fees are final except where required by law.

---

## 3. Printed Products

Printed card packs may be eligible for refunds or reprints under the conditions below.

### 3.1 Manufacturing Defects

We will reprint or refund orders that arrive with:

- Misaligned cuts
- Incorrect colors compared to the approved proof
- Damaged or bent cards
- Printing artifacts or smudges
- Missing or duplicated cards

You must notify us within **14 days** of delivery and provide photos of the issue.

### 3.2 User Design Errors

We do **not** offer refunds or reprints for:

- Typos in card text
- Incorrect images uploaded by the user
- Poor‑quality images provided by the user
- Design choices the user later regrets
- Cards printed exactly as approved in the digital proof

Users are responsible for reviewing their design before minting or printing.

### 3.3 Shipping Issues

If an order is lost or damaged in transit:

- We will work with the carrier to resolve the issue
- Replacement shipments may be issued at our discretion

---

## 4. Subscriptions

Subscription fees are **non‑refundable**, including:

- Monthly or annual charges
- Partial billing periods
- Unused subscription time

You may cancel at any time, and your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

---

## 5. Chargebacks

Filing a chargeback for valid digital goods or fulfilled print orders may result in:

- Account suspension
- Loss of access to minted cards
- Cancellation of pending orders

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact us first so we can resolve it quickly.

---

## 6. How to Request a Refund or Reprint

To request a refund or reprint for eligible issues, contact:

**support@legendaryprofiles.com**

Include:

- Your order number
- A description of the issue
- Photos (for printed product defects)

We typically respond within 2–3 business days.

---

## 7. Policy Updates

We may update this Refund Policy periodically.  
Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
