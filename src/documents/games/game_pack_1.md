## About this Game

A fun party game for all ages! Match the best card to the prompt and see who wins each round.

- 4–10 players
- 20–30 minutes
- Includes 200 cards

## How to Play

1. Each player draws 5 cards.
2. The judge reads a prompt card.
3. Players submit their best match.
4. Judge picks the winner!

Enjoy hours of laughter and creativity.
