## About this Game

A cooperative adventure game. Explore dungeons, defeat monsters, and collect treasure!

- 1–5 players
- 45–90 minutes
- Includes 150 cards, dice, and tokens

## How to Play

1. Choose your hero and gather your party.
2. Explore the dungeon by drawing cards.
3. Battle monsters and avoid traps.
4. Collect loot and escape alive!

Perfect for game night and fantasy fans.
