Greetings! My name is Adrian, and I am the lead developer behind Legendary Profiles. In this blog, I hope to give you a better sense of what we are trying to build, and how you can become part of this journey.

## What the Heck Are We Doing?

One of the first questions I get whenever I show someone Legendary Profiles is:

**_"Adrian, why are you doing this?"_**

It's a fair question.

There are already dozens of companies out there that will happily print you a business card. They offer countless templates, paper types, finishes, and design tools. If you spend enough time searching online, you'll even find cards with NFC chips, QR codes, fold-out designs, and all sorts of twists that make me wish that I had just _half_ the creativity needed to make something similar.

So why make something new? For us, the answer came from years of frustration with traditional business cards.

No matter which service I tried, the experience always felt limiting. I would spend hours searching through templates trying to find something that matched our style, only to settle for the least-bad option. I struggled to fit the information I cared about onto a tiny piece of cardstock while being forced to make room for fields and social platforms I didn't even use.

### Here Are Your Cards (and they're Already Out of Date) . . .

And even when we finally got everything looking right, the card was already becoming outdated.

As a career military officer, I know that life is full of uncertainty. Phone numbers change. Email addresses change. Jobs/assignments change. Websites change. Yet the moment a traditional card is printed, it's frozen in time. We've all ordered cards that became obsolete after a career change, experimented with NFC solutions that were too expensive to hand out freely, and watched perfectly good cards become useless simply because our circumstances changed.

To add salt to the wound: most business cards stop being useful the moment they've been handed out.

At the end of the day, they're just a way to exchange information.

We wanted to create something more.

### Making More than a Card

The truth is that our team is made up of computer scientists, electrical engineers, software developers, and lifelong geeks. When we're not writing code, we're usually playing games, talking about games, or thinking about what makes games engaging. We love systems, progression, storytelling, customization, and shared experiences.

Eventually, we started asking ourselves a different question:

What if a business card wasn't just a business card?

What if it was an identity?

What if it could grow and evolve alongside its owner? What if it could tell a story, showcase accomplishments, and create opportunities for people to engage with one another in ways that go beyond simply exchanging contact information?

That's the idea behind Legendary Profiles.

### Our Mission

We're not trying to build a better printing company. We're building a platform where a card becomes the gateway to a living profile and a more meaningful introduction. Yes, it's still a business card (and a pretty cool one at that). But it's a card that allows networking to be personal, dynamic, and maybe even a little fun.

To our knowledge, nobody has really tried to turn a business card into an experience before.

Maybe that's because it takes a particular kind of nerd to look at a business card and see the beginnings of a character sheet.

Fortunately, we're exactly those nerds.

## Thank You for Reading

If you've made it this far, thank you. After all, you're reading a blog post on a business card website, which is probably not how most people planned to spend their day. Legendary Profiles is still in its early stages, and we're just beginning the journey of turning this idea into something real. We'll need the support, feedback, and encouragement of our community to make that happen.

In the weeks ahead, we plan to share more about our vision, our design decisions, our successes, and the lessons we learn along the way. We hope you'll stick around and join us as we try to turn what started as a cool idea between a group of nerds into something genuinely legendary.
