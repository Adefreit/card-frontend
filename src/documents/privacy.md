# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## 1. Introduction

Legendary Profiles (“we,” “us,” “our”) provides a platform where users can design trading‑card‑style profile cards, mint digital proofs, generate QR codes, access analytics, and optionally purchase printed cards.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have regarding your data.

By using the service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you provide directly, including:

- Account details (name, email, password)
- Card content (text, images, artwork)
- Contact links for your QR hub
- Shipping information for printed orders
- Payment information (processed by third‑party providers)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the platform, we may automatically collect:

- IP address
- Device type and browser
- Usage logs
- Session data
- Cookies and tracking data

### 2.3 QR Code Scan Data

When someone scans your card’s QR code, we may collect:

- Approximate location (city‑level)
- Device type
- Timestamp
- Link clicks

Analytics are aggregated and anonymized for subscribers.

### 2.4 Payment Information

Payments are processed by third‑party providers. We do not store full credit card numbers.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide and maintain the service
- Generate and manage your cards, QR codes, and link hubs
- Process minting, subscription, and printing transactions
- Provide analytics to subscribers
- Improve platform performance and features
- Communicate with you about updates or support
- Prevent fraud and ensure platform security

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. User‑Generated Content

You retain ownership of the content you upload or create.

By using the platform, you grant us permission to:

- Store your content
- Display it to you and anyone you share it with
- Print it (if ordered)
- Generate QR codes and metadata
- Use anonymized versions for product improvement or showcases

You are responsible for ensuring you have rights to all uploaded content.

## 5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Maintain sessions
- Improve site performance
- Analyze usage
- Support login and security features

You may disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not function properly.

## 6. Sharing Your Information

We may share information with:

- Payment processors

- Legal authorities (if required)

We retain:
