# Subscription Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## 1. Overview

Legendary Profiles offers optional subscription plans that provide enhanced features beyond the free tier. This Subscription Policy explains how subscriptions work, what benefits they include, how billing is handled, and how cancellations take effect.

By subscribing, you agree to this policy as part of our Terms of Service.

---

## 2. Subscription Benefits

Subscription features may include:

- Editable link hubs for your QR code
- Access to analytics dashboards
- Additional customization options
- Priority feature access
- Optional free monthly mints (if offered)

Features may vary by plan and may change over time as the platform evolves.

---

## 3. Billing & Renewal

Subscriptions are billed through thirdparty payment processors.

### 3.1 Billing Cycles

You may choose:

- **Monthly billing**
- **Annual billing** (discounted rate)

Your subscription will renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle unless canceled.

### 3.2 Payment Information

You must provide accurate and uptodate payment information.  
If a payment fails, we may:

- Retry the charge
- Suspend subscription features
- Cancel the subscription

---

## 4. Free Monthly Mints (If Applicable)

Some subscription plans may include free monthly mints.

### 4.1 Usage Rules

- Free mints do **not** roll over to future months
- Free mints apply only to the current billing cycle
- Unused free mints are forfeited at the end of the cycle
